Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 17, 2026

About this policy

Omnificology operates Handy to manage service inquiries, handyman and fabrication work, bookings, and customer communications. This policy explains how Omnificology handles personal information through Handy and its Thumbtack and messaging integrations. It applies to customers, people making inquiries, and users who authorize the integration.

Information and sources

We collect information you provide through service inquiries, booking forms, accounts, and communications, and information received through connected services such as Thumbtack. Depending on your interaction and the permissions granted, this may include your name, phone number, email, service address, project descriptions and photos, requested availability, estimates, appointments, lead identifiers, and conversation history. Account and technical records may include an authentication identifier, authorization credentials for connected accounts, IP addresses, request logs, message identifiers, timestamps, and delivery status. Please avoid sending sensitive information that is unnecessary for your project.

How information is used

We use information to respond to inquiries, assess project requirements, prepare estimates and responses, schedule and provide services, maintain customer and job records, process service-related communications, and troubleshoot and protect the integration. Thumbtack lead and conversation data can be linked to Handy customer and job records. Where configured, appointment and contact details are processed through Google Calendar and Google Contacts, and operational notifications are sent to the business.

AI-assisted processing

Google Cloud/Gemini is used to analyze lead details and conversations, summarize work, identify scheduling information, and assist business follow-up. AI-assisted customer response features, if enabled, may process relevant inquiry and message content to prepare or send responses. AI output can be inaccurate; an automated assessment or response does not itself guarantee a quote, appointment, or service commitment. The current lead-processing workflow also supports internal summaries and scheduling assistance. Do not submit information you do not want processed for these purposes.

SMS and Telnyx

When SMS is used, Telnyx and telecommunications carriers process phone numbers, message content, message identifiers, timestamps, and delivery information to route messages and report delivery. Messages relate to service inquiries and customer communications; authorizing the integration is not consent to unrelated promotional messages. You can ask Omnificology to stop SMS communications at the contact below. Where supported by the messaging service, you may also reply STOP to opt out or HELP for assistance. Message and data rates may apply, frequency varies, and delivery is not guaranteed. Opting out of SMS does not delete existing business records or revoke a separate Thumbtack authorization.

Storage, retention, and security

Handy uses Google Firebase/Firestore and related cloud services to store account, customer, job, lead, and message records. We use authentication and access controls to restrict access to business records. No storage or transmission method is completely secure. Retention depends on the purpose of the record, the service relationship, operational needs, and applicable legal, accounting, and dispute-resolution obligations. There is no single fixed retention period stated for all records. Records may remain after an inquiry ends or authorization is revoked; revocation is not an automatic deletion request. Contact us to request deletion or ask about a particular record. Copies in backups and records held independently by service providers may be subject to their own retention processes.

Disclosures and service providers

Information is disclosed as needed to provide and operate the services: to Thumbtack for authorized lead and conversation functions; Telnyx and carriers for SMS; Google/Firebase for hosting, storage, authentication, AI processing, calendar, and contact functions; and other providers used for the particular service you request, such as payment or invoicing services. Access may also be provided to people assisting Omnificology in performing your requested work, as needed for that work. Information may be disclosed to comply with legal obligations, respond to valid legal requests, protect people or systems, or resolve disputes. Connected services also handle information under their own policies. This integration is intended for service management and communications, not resale of lead data.

Your choices and privacy requests

You can request access, correction, or deletion of your information, ask about retention or disclosures, and request that communications stop by emailing shane@omnificology.com. Depending on where you live, applicable law may provide additional rights, including objection to or restriction of processing. We may need to verify your identity and authority before acting on a request, and some records may need to be retained for lawful business or legal reasons. You can revoke connected-account permissions through the provider’s available account controls or contact us for help disconnecting. To exercise rights over information held independently by Thumbtack, Telnyx, or another provider, contact that provider as well.

Website storage and changes

Handy uses authentication and browser storage, including service-worker caching, to support sign-in and site operation. This policy may be updated as the service changes; the effective date identifies the published version. For questions about this policy or personal information, contact Omnificology at shane@omnificology.com.
